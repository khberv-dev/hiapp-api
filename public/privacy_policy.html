1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes the manner in which HiApp ("HiApp", the "Company", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores, protects and discloses personal information of individuals who use the HiApp mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By accessing or using the Service, you ("User" or "you") acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and consent to the processing of your personal information as described herein. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, you must not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Account Information. When you register for or sign in to the Service using a Google account or an Apple account, we collect and store the following information made available to us by the respective provider with your authorization:

  • your first name;
  • your last name;
  • your email address; and
  • your profile image (avatar) associated with your Google or Apple account.

2.2. User-Provided Content. Where you elect to upload a profile image (avatar) to the Service, we collect and store such image.

2.3. Conversation Data. In order to provide the voice conversation functionality of the Service, audio captured by your device's microphone during an active conversation is transmitted to our servers and processed in order to generate a text transcript and a spoken response. Text transcripts of conversations, together with the date, time and duration of each conversation, are stored on our servers. Raw audio recordings are not retained after processing.

2.4. Subscription and Payment Information. Where you purchase a subscription, we store records of the selected plan, the amount, the payment status and the subscription period. Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor and are not received or stored by us.

2.5. We do not knowingly collect any information other than as set out in this Section 2.

3. Purposes of Processing

We process the information described above solely for the following purposes:

  • to create, authenticate and maintain your account;
  • to provide, operate and maintain the Service, including the voice conversation functionality and the continuity of conversations;
  • to administer subscriptions, process payments and enforce usage limits applicable to your account;
  • to ensure the security, integrity and proper functioning of the Service; and
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1. All personal information collected through the Service is stored on servers controlled by the Company. We do not store your personal information on third-party advertising or analytics platforms.

4.2. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction. Such measures include, without limitation, encryption of data in transit, access controls restricting access to personal information to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, and token-based authentication of all requests to the Service.

4.3. Notwithstanding the foregoing, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is entirely secure, and the Company cannot guarantee the absolute security of personal information.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We disclose personal information to third parties only to the extent strictly necessary to provide the Service, as follows:

  • Authentication providers (Google LLC and Apple Inc.), for the purpose of verifying your identity when you sign in;
  • Speech and language processing providers (Google LLC), which process conversation audio and text on our behalf in order to produce transcripts and responses; and
  • Payment processors (Multicard), which process subscription payments.

Each such third party processes information in accordance with its own privacy policy. We may further disclose personal information where required to do so by law, regulation, court order or other legal process, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of the Company, its users or the public.

6. Voice Data and Third-Party Processing

6.1. Collection of Voice Data. The voice conversation functionality of the Service requires access to your device's microphone. Audio is captured only while a conversation is active and only after you have granted microphone permission on your device. You may withdraw such permission at any time through your device settings, in which case the voice conversation functionality will be unavailable.

6.2. Transmission to Third-Party Service Providers. To analyze and process your voice input and to generate responses, the Service transmits the following data to Google LLC through the Google Gemini API, acting as our third-party service provider:

  • audio captured during an active conversation, which is streamed in real time for the purpose of speech recognition (conversion of speech to text);
  • text transcripts of your utterances, together with preceding messages of the same conversation, for the purpose of analyzing your input and generating a response; and
  • the text of generated responses, for the purpose of converting such text into synthesized speech.

6.3. Limitations. Data transmitted under Section 6.2 is limited to the content of your conversations. Your name, email address and profile image are not transmitted to the speech and language processing provider. We do not authorize such provider to use the data for any purpose other than providing its services to us, save as permitted under its applicable terms of service.

6.4. Retention by the Provider. We do not retain raw audio recordings after processing. Data transmitted to Google LLC is processed and may be temporarily retained by Google LLC in accordance with the Gemini API terms of service and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), which govern its handling of such data.

6.5. International Transfer. The servers of our third-party service providers may be located outside your country of residence. By using the voice conversation functionality, you acknowledge that your voice data and related transcripts may be transferred to and processed in such jurisdictions, subject to appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

6.6. Consent. By initiating a voice conversation, you expressly consent to the transmission and processing of your voice data as described in this Section 6.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide the Service to you. Upon deletion of your account, your account information, profile image and conversation transcripts are permanently deleted from our servers, save for any records that we are required to retain in order to comply with legal, accounting or tax obligations.
